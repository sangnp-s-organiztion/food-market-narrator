# CHUC NANG SELLER

## 1. Quan ly diem thuyet minh (POI)

Seller la cac chu quan an tai pho am thuc Vinh Khanh. Seller co the tao va quan ly thong tin diem thuyet minh (POI) cua cua hang minh de hien thi trong ung dung.

### Thong tin POI bao gom

- **Ten dia diem**
- **Mo ta van ban**
  - Gioi thieu quan
  - Mon an noi bat
  - Dac diem dac trung cua quan
- **Anh minh hoa**
  - Hinh anh mon an
  - Hinh anh quan
- **Link ban do**
  - Vi tri quan tren ban do
- **File audio hoac script TTS**
  - Upload file audio thuyet minh
  - Hoac nhap noi dung de he thong Text-to-Speech doc tu dong

### Cac thao tac chinh

- Tao POI moi
- Chinh sua thong tin POI
- Cap nhat hinh anh
- Cap nhat noi dung mo ta
- Upload audio hoac nhap script TTS
- Xem truoc noi dung thuyet minh

## 2. Thong ke du lieu cho cua hang

Seller co the xem mot so du lieu thong ke lien quan den POI cua minh:

- So luot nguoi dung nghe thuyet minh
- Thoi gian trung binh nguoi dung nghe noi dung
- Tan suat xuat hien cua POI trong hanh trinh nguoi dung

### Muc dich

- Giup chu quan hieu muc do quan tam cua khach
- Cai thien noi dung thuyet minh va hinh anh

## User Personas

Phan nay mo ta cac nhom nguoi dung chinh cua he thong va nhu cau cua ho khi su dung ung dung thuyet minh tu dong tai pho am thuc Vinh Khanh.

### 1. Du khach / Khach tham quan (Visitor)

**Do tuoi:** 18 - 40  
**Thiet bi su dung:** Smartphone (Android / iOS)

**Dac diem:**

- Thuong la khach du lich hoac nguoi lan dau den pho am thuc Vinh Khanh.
- Thich kham pha cac mon an moi va cac quan noi tieng.
- Di chuyen trong khu pho va tim kiem cac quan an dang thu.

**Nhu cau:**

- Biet duoc quan an nao noi bat trong khu vuc.
- Nghe gioi thieu nhanh ve mon an hoac quan khi di ngang qua.
- Co thong tin co ban nhu mon dac trung, gia, vi tri.

**Pain points:**

- Khong biet quan nao ngon hoac dang thu.
- Kho tim vi tri quan noi tieng trong khu pho dong duc.
- Thieu thong tin ve mon an truoc khi quyet dinh mua.

---

### 2. Nguoi quan ly / Admin

**Do tuoi:** 22 - 40  
**Thiet bi su dung:** May tinh hoac tablet

**Dac diem:**

- Quan ly noi dung cua he thong.
- Chiu trach nhiem cap nhat thong tin ve quan an va noi dung thuyet minh.

**Nhu cau:**

- Them moi cac dia diem hoac quan an vao he thong.
- Chinh sua thong tin nhu mo ta, menu, noi dung thuyet minh.
- Quan ly du lieu dia diem mot cach de dang.

**Pain points:**

- Can mot he thong quan ly don gian va de su dung.
- Phai dam bao thong tin hien thi cho nguoi dung luon chinh xac va cap nhat.

---

### 3. Chu quan / Nguoi ban (Seller)

**Do tuoi:** 20 - 50  
**Thiet bi su dung:** Smartphone hoac may tinh

**Dac diem:**

- La chu quan hoac nguoi quan ly quan an trong khu pho.
- Muon quang ba quan an va mon dac trung cua minh.

**Nhu cau:**

- Quan cua minh duoc gioi thieu cho nhieu khach hon.
- Co the cap nhat menu hoac thong tin quan.

**Pain points:**

- Kho tiep can khach du lich moi.
- Canh tranh voi nhieu quan khac trong khu vuc.

## User Stories

Phan nay mo ta cac hanh vi chinh cua nguoi dung khi su dung ung dung thuyet minh tu dong tai pho am thuc Vinh Khanh. Cac user stories duoc viet theo dinh dang:

**As a [user]**  
**I want [action]**  
**So that [goal]**

---

### 1. Visitor / Du khach

**Story 1**

As a visitor  
I want to scan a QR code  
So that I can quickly open the food market narrator app.

**Story 2**

As a visitor  
I want the app to detect my location  
So that I can receive information about nearby food stalls.

**Story 3**

As a visitor  
I want the app to automatically play narration when I approach a location  
So that I can learn about the food stall or dish without searching manually.

**Story 4**

As a visitor  
I want to view information about a food stall  
So that I can decide whether I want to try the food there.

**Story 5**

As a visitor  
I want to see recommended food stalls nearby  
So that I can discover popular or famous dishes.

---

### 2. Seller / Chu quan

**Story 6**

As a seller  
I want to update information about my food stall  
So that visitors can see accurate information about my menu.

**Story 7**

As a seller  
I want my stall to be introduced through narration  
So that more visitors can discover my food.

---

### 3. Admin / Quan tri he thong

**Story 8**

As an admin  
I want to add new food stall locations  
So that the system can provide narration for more places.

**Story 9**

As an admin  
I want to update narration content  
So that visitors always receive accurate and updated information.

**Story 10**

As an admin  
I want to manage all locations and narration data  
So that the system operates correctly and efficiently.
